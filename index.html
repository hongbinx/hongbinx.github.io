01 / Bio

Hongbin Xu is a recording artist, keyboardist, music director and producer based in Los Angeles. He earned a bachelor of music in keyboard performance in contemporary styles, with a minor in audio production, from the Musicians Institute in 2023. Since then, he has supported artists on stage and in studio.
